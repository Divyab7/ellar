Your 5 days

15Tue

Fly to Bangkok, drive to Pattaya

Overnight flight from Bangalore. Our representative meets you at Bangkok airport and the coach takes you to Pattaya, about 2.5 hours. Rooms are requested early so you can rest. Evening free by the sea, with the Alcazar cabaret show as an option.

Dinner arranged for the family with Indian and vegetarian choices.

16Wed

Coral Island by private speedboat

A 30-minute boat ride to Coral Island for swimming, sunbeds and optional water sports. Thai lunch on the island, back in Pattaya by 3 pm. Evening free at the night market.

Breakfast and lunch included.

If the sea is rough: the day becomes Nong Nooch Tropical Garden with the elephant and cultural show, at no extra cost.
17Thu

Bangkok temples

Drive to Bangkok. Afternoon at Wat Traimit, home of the solid Golden Buddha, and Wat Pho with the 46-metre Reclining Buddha. Check in, then Pratunam market is a short walk away. Shoulders and knees covered for the temples; we carry spare wraps.

Breakfast included.

18Fri

Safari World and Marine Park

Drive through the safari park in our own coach, then the dolphin, sea lion, orangutan and stunt shows, all under cover. Buffet lunch inside the park. Evening option: dinner cruise on the Chao Phraya river past the lit-up temples.

Breakfast and lunch included.

19Sat

Fly home

Free morning for last shopping at Platinum Mall next door. Late check-out requested, then a private transfer to the airport for the evening flight to Bangalore.

Breakfast included.
