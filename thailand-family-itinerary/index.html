The journey

Two cities, one rhythm

Beach first, city second. You arrive tired off a night flight, so the itinerary starts slow by the sea and builds to Bangkok's temples, shows and shopping once everyone has found their feet.

Program

Five days, hour by hour

Every day has its timings, meals, a rain plan where it matters, and a note on how I'll look after the group that day.

15Sep · Tue
Day 1 · Arrival

Bangalore → Bangkok → Pattaya

Night flight, coach to the coast, first evening by the sea
BLD
~00:30Depart Bangalore. Group check-in together, three hours before. Final timings on ticketing.
~06:00Arrive Suvarnabhumi (BKK). Immigration with your arrival card already filed. A Lurws representative meets you at the hall with a name board.
07:30Private coach to Pattaya, about 2.5 hours, with a breakfast stop on the way at own cost.
10:30Sanctuary of Truth (optional, about THB 500 direct). An all-teak temple carved by hand on the seafront, the first family photo of the trip.
14:00Check in at the Pattaya hotel. Early check-in requested for the group. Afternoon free to rest.
18:30Alcazar Cabaret Show (optional add-on), 70 minutes, then dinner near Beach Road.
Coordinator's noteEveryone lands tired. I keep the morning to one stop and get the rooms early. Dinner is booked in advance for 12 with Indian and vegetarian options, so nobody is hunting for food on night one.
OptionalAlcazar and Nong Nooch are in the package builder below. Nong Nooch can move to today if the family wants a quieter Day 2.
16Sep · Wed
Day 2 · Beach

Pattaya → Coral Island (Koh Larn)

Private speedboat, beach and island lunch, evening market
BLD
07:00Breakfast at the hotel.
08:30Speedboat to Coral Island, reserved for the group, about 30 minutes each way. Life jackets on board.
09:15Beach time. Sunbeds, swimming and optional water sports at direct payment: parasailing, banana boat, sea walk, jet ski.
12:30Thai lunch on the island, included. Vegetarian dishes confirmed in advance.
15:00Back to Pattaya before the afternoon showers. Free time at the hotel pool.
19:00Evening free at Walking Street or Pattaya Night Market. Coach drop and pick-up at a fixed time.
Rain planIf boats are held by the marine office, today becomes Nong Nooch Tropical Garden with the cultural and elephant show. Decision by 07:30 with the family, no extra cost when it replaces the island.
Coordinator's noteWater sports are paid to the operator directly. I share the fair going rates the evening before so nobody overpays, and I keep the group's phones and wallets in one dry bag during the swim.
17Sep · Thu
Day 3 · Temples

Pattaya → Bangkok city and temples

Golden Buddha, Reclining Buddha, Pratunam evening
BLD
07:30Breakfast, bags in the lobby by 08:45.
09:00Check out and drive to Bangkok.
11:30Gems Gallery stop, 30 to 40 minutes. A showroom visit with no obligation to buy. Lunch nearby at own cost.
14:00Wat Traimit, the 5.5-tonne solid Golden Buddha in Chinatown. Entry included.
15:15Wat Pho, the 46-metre Reclining Buddha and Thailand's oldest massage school. Entry included. Tour ends 16:30.
17:30Check in at the Bangkok hotel.
19:00Evening free at Pratunam Market and Indra Square, a short walk from the Pratunam hotels.
Temple dress codeShoulders and knees covered, shoes off inside the halls. I remind the group the evening before and carry spare sarongs on the coach so nobody is turned away at the gate.
On requestGrand Palace can be added the same afternoon (THB 500 entry, closed on some royal ceremony days). It replaces the Gems Gallery stop to keep the timing.
18Sep · Fri
Day 4 · Safari

Safari World and Marine Park

Drive-through safari, four shows, buffet lunch, river cruise evening
BLD
07:00Breakfast at the hotel.
08:30Depart for Safari World, Minburi, about an hour from the city.
09:30Safari Park drive-through in our own coach: giraffes, zebras, tigers and bears from the window.
10:45Marine Park shows as per the day's schedule: orangutan boxing, dolphins, sea lions and the Hollywood stunt show.
12:30International buffet lunch inside the park, included. Vegetarian counter available.
16:30Return to the hotel.
19:30Chao Phraya Princess dinner cruise (optional add-on): two hours past Wat Arun and the Grand Palace lit up, buffet and live music.
Coordinator's noteShow timings change daily. I confirm the schedule at the gate and walk the group from arena to arena so the older members never rush. This is the day for the big family photo.
Rain planEvery show is under cover and the safari is from inside the coach, so this day runs in any weather.
19Sep · Sat
Day 5 · Home

Bangkok → Bangalore

Last shopping, late check-out, evening flight
BLD
08:00Breakfast. Morning free for Platinum Mall or Big C, next door to Pratunam.
12:00Check out. Late check-out requested; bags held with the concierge if not available.
D − 3.5hPrivate transfer to BKK. Group check-in together. VAT refund counter for shopping above THB 2,000 per shop.
EveningFly to Bangalore, arriving the same night. Timings confirmed on ticketing.
Coordinator's noteI collect a quick feedback note at the airport and share the family photo album within 48 hours of return.
Want a free day in Bangkok?The 6-day option under Terms turns Saturday into Chatuchak Market and ICONSIAM, flying home Sunday.
