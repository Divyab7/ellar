Good to know

Before you fly

Passport valid till at least 19 March 2027. Copies of all 12 are needed with the deposit.
Entry is visa-free for Indian passports. The Thailand Digital Arrival Card is filed for you three days before travel.
Carry THB 10,000 per person in cash or card. Immigration may ask. 1 THB is about ₹ 2.65.
Weather is 27–32 °C with short afternoon showers. Light cottons, an umbrella, quick-dry footwear.
Temple day needs shoulders and knees covered. Shoes come off inside.
Phones: a Thai SIM or eSIM at the airport is about THB 300. Keep one phone on Indian roaming for OTPs.
