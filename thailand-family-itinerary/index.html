Pre-departure

Before you fly

A short list for every member of the family. I walk the group through all of it on the briefing call and check documents one by one before the airport.

Documents

  • Passport valid till at least 19 March 2027 (6 months beyond return)
  • Indian passport holders enter visa-free; no visa fee today
  • Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC), filed online within 72 hours before arrival, so on 12 Sep or later. I file it for the whole group.
  • Return ticket and hotel confirmation printouts
  • Proof of funds: at least THB 10,000 per person in cash or card, which immigration may check

Money and phone

  • THB is roughly ₹ 2.6 – 2.7; exchange a little in Bangalore, the rest at Super Rich counters in Bangkok for a better rate
  • Cards work in malls and hotels; markets, water sports and tuk-tuks are cash
  • An eSIM or a Thai SIM at the airport (about THB 300 for 8 days unlimited); keep one phone on Indian roaming for OTPs
  • Family WhatsApp group with the guide and the Lurws support number

Monsoon packing

  • Light cottons, a compact umbrella or poncho, quick-dry footwear
  • Temple wear: a long trouser or skirt and a covered top for Day 3
  • Swimwear, a rash guard and reef-safe sunscreen for Coral Island
  • Regular medicines in hand baggage with the prescription, motion-sickness tablets for the speedboat
  • Universal adaptor: Thailand uses 220V, Type A, B and C plugs
