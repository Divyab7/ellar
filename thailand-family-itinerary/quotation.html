Program in brief

15SEP · TUE

Bangalore – Bangkok – Pattaya

Overnight flight from Bangalore, early-morning arrival at BKK. Meet and greet by the Lurws representative. Private coach to Pattaya (approx. 2.5 hrs) with an optional stop at the Sanctuary of Truth (THB 500 direct). Early check-in requested. Evening free; Alcazar Cabaret Show as an add-on. Dinner for 12 arranged with Indian and vegetarian options.

Meals: –

16SEP · WED

Pattaya – Coral Island (Koh Larn)

08:30 private speedboat for the group, about 30 minutes. Beach time, optional water sports at direct payment, Thai lunch on the island. Return by 15:00. Evening free at Walking Street / Pattaya Night Market.

Meals: B, L

Weather alternative: Nong Nooch Tropical Garden with the cultural and elephant show, at no extra cost if boats are suspended.
17SEP · THU

Pattaya – Bangkok city and temple tour

09:00 check-out and drive to Bangkok. Gems Gallery stop en route. City tour 14:00 – 16:30: Wat Traimit (Golden Buddha) and Wat Pho (Reclining Buddha), entries included. Dress code: shoulders and knees covered; spare wraps carried. Check-in by 17:30. Evening free for shopping at Pratunam / Indra Square.

Meals: B

18SEP · FRI

Bangkok – Safari World and Marine Park

08:30 departure. Safari drive-through in the private coach, then the orangutan, dolphin, sea lion and stunt shows as per park schedule, all under cover. Buffet lunch inside the park. Return 16:30. Evening: Chao Phraya Princess Dinner Cruise as an add-on.

Meals: B, L

19SEP · SAT

Bangkok – Bangalore

Free morning for shopping at Platinum Mall. Check-out by 12:00 (late check-out requested). Private transfer to BKK 3.5 hours before departure. Fly to Bangalore, arriving the same night.

Meals: B
